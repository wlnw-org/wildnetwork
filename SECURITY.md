# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue. Use GitHub's private reporting:
**Security tab, then "Report a vulnerability"** on https://github.com/wlnw-org/wildnetwork.

You should get a first reply within seven days.

## Reporting a sensitive location

If the map shows a record that could put an animal or a person at risk (a nest or den of a threatened species, a private home shown too precisely), report it the same private way. Include the species, the place and the date. Such records are hidden first and discussed afterwards.

## In scope

- The public site and API at wildnetwork.arunrajiah.com
- Device key registration and the ingest endpoint
- The installer served at `/agent/install.sh`

## Out of scope

- Volume based denial of service
- Problems in upstream data sources (report those to the source)
