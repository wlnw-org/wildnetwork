# Governance

## Purpose

WildNetwork is an open platform for seeing how birds and animals move across the world. It is run in the open, for the benefit of wildlife and the people who study and protect it.

## Roles

- **Maintainer:** [Arun Rajiah](https://www.arunrajiah.com) (@arunrajiah). Sets direction, reviews and merges, operates the public site at wlnw.org, and has the final say when agreement cannot be reached.
- **Committers:** contributors invited by the maintainer after a sustained record of good work. They can review and merge.
- **Contributors:** everyone who opens an issue, a pull request, or runs a device that shares data.
- **Science advisers:** ecologists and data partners who review how movement is measured and presented. Their review is sought on any change to the insight methods.

## Decisions

- Ordinary changes: a reviewed pull request.
- Changes to how insights are computed, to what data is stored, to privacy defaults, or to data licensing: an issue open for at least seven days, then a written decision in that issue.
- Disagreements: discussed in the issue. If no agreement is reached, the maintainer decides and records why.

## Commitments

These hold regardless of who funds or operates the project.

1. **The code stays open.** This repository remains under AGPL-3.0. The capture agent remains under Apache 2.0, and the WDX format under MIT.
2. **The read API stays open.** Data that contributors share with an open license remains readable without payment.
3. **Contributors keep their rights.** People who send detections choose the license, and can ask for their station's data to be removed.
4. **Attribution is kept.** Every source is credited where its data is shown.
5. **Sensitive locations are protected.** Precision is never increased beyond what a source or contributor provided.
6. **Honest insights.** Methods are documented, and known limits are stated next to the result.

## Sustainability

Running a global service costs money. The project may accept sponsorship, grants and infrastructure credits, and may later offer paid services around the open platform, for example hosted private instances, support, or custom analysis. Such services do not change the commitments above. Sponsors do not get control over the roadmap, the data, or the methods. Funding sources are listed publicly.

## Names and marks

"WildNetwork", "WDX" and "wdx-agent", and the WildNetwork logo, identify this project. The license lets you run and modify the software, including commercially under its terms. Please do not present a modified or separately hosted version as the official WildNetwork.
